## Q2 Decontamination for the next build (v5)

**What E_val covered** [V, `gold-side/summary.json`]: sources = only the 23,819 MMLU/CSQA train rows; targets = every val row (18,428) and every held-out row (39,887) of every family. So E_val is complete for MMLU/CSQA-sourced contamination of any val row and **silent on code, CLINC and SQuAD train rows as sources**. 1,196 train rows hit val, 0 hit held-out; `max_pairs: 400` semantics [U].

**The rule, end to end.**
1. **Keys.** (i) exact `identity_key`/`content_hash` equality across splits (the splitter's, already); (ii) near: word 8-gram containment ≥ 0.5 of the *target* row's n-grams over rendered `prompt_content`, blake2b-64 hashed — `qd_train.replay.decontaminate`'s definition (`python/qd_train/replay.py:169-233`), n and threshold unchanged; (iii) the MinHash Jaccard ≥ 0.8 near-dup check stays (weaker, GAP-DATA-GENERAL-CE-ROWS-OVERLAP-FAMILY-VAL).
2. **Sources × targets.** The list is applied after split assignment and touches rows already assigned to train only — a val or held-out row never moves (rule 2). Every train row of every family, gold or replay-drawn, against (a) **every val row of every family: refuse-on-hit → exclude the train row**; (b) held-out in two classes: the repo-disjoint held-out splits of trainable families → exclude; the two task-holdout families (`code.language_id`, `qa.answerability`) overlap train content by construction (222/234, GAP-PORTED-HELDOUT-FAMILY-CONTENT-OVERLAPS-TRAIN) → **report per (source family, held-out family), enforce nothing, and the attestation names them unenforced** — content-disjointness there changes a held-out population (rule 2, the human's). Report val×held-out overlap too. The OOD prose cases are val MMLU/CSQA questions (`python/qd_train/ood.py:10-12`), so (a) covers them; the needle fillers are hard-coded templates (`needle.py:52-55`) and scrambled/unseen-language are synthetic — no train-vs-suite scan; the suite's design is G12.

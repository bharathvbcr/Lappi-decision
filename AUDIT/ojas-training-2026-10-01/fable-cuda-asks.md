Cost: 3 days on 40, and inline PTX conflicts with the hiprtc-reuse comment at `ojas-kernels/src/source.rs:1-2` — a comment on a crate with no HIP kernels (`ojas-hip` is a copy probe), so a later `#ifdef` fallback is the honest answer, not a reason to adopt cuBLAS.

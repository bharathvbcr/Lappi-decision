That is ordinary f32 accumulation growth over a 2048-long dot product (bound √H·ε₃₂ = 5.4e-6; measured 1.2e-6). The Rust head accumulates every reduction in f64 and rounds once (`crates/qd-train/src/span_head.rs:47-54`, `dot` at 336-340), which is why it sits under one ulp of the exact value.

**1. Reference and tolerance.** The reference for the span head's rung (a) gate is the oracle's **float64 arm** (`ref64_*`: the same `SpanPointerHead` `.double()`, same plan, CPU). The tolerance is **unchanged**: max|rust − ref64| ≤ 1e-6 · max|ref64| per tensor, loss |rust − ref64| ≤ 1e-6·|ref64|, all 9 cases. The "f32" in "≤1e-6 rel (f32, host)" named the head's storage dtype, not the oracle's; this is a reference clarification, not a threshold move. Under it the Rust head's worst tensor is 1.418e-7, and h2048-point is not borderline (3.033e-8) — its borderline-ness was an artifact of the wrong reference.

**2. The fp32 arm: report-only pin, not a gate.** A gate derived from torch's own error (1e-6 + `torch_f32_vs_f64`) is implied by gate B through the triangle inequality: rel(rust, t32) ≤ (rel(rust, f64) + rel(t32, f64)) · max|f64|/max|t32|, and that ratio is 1 ± 1.2e-6. It is a check that cannot fail when gate B passes, which is exactly the failure mode CLAUDE.md names. So the test keeps printing rust-vs-fp32 and fp32-vs-f64 per tensor (as the audit file does now) and asserts one fixture-integrity fact instead: the manifest's `torch_f32_vs_f64` equals the value recomputed from the two dumps. That catches one arm regenerated without the other. A bound on torch's own fp32 error (to catch an oracle regenerated on MPS or in bf16) would be a separate fixture-sanity choice for the human, not part of this decision.

**Rationale.** The tolerance I set was meant to bound the Rust head's distance from the true function, and torch fp32 was only ever a stand-in for that function; at H ≤ 96 the stand-in is good to 1–4 ulps and the distinction is invisible, but at the real width its own rounding reaches 11 ulps of max, so a 1e-6 comparison against it measures whether two independent roundings happen to align rather than whether the Rust head is right, and h2048-point's 9.67e-7 "pass" is as meaningless as h2048-abstain-duplicate's 1.216e-6 "fail". The fixtures already carry the exact value, the Rust head is within one ulp of it on every tensor, and the gate at 1e-6 against that value is both tighter in meaning and unchanged in number; keeping the fp32 arm as a report-only pin preserves the evidence that the campaign's actual dtype is within f32 rounding of the same function without manufacturing a gate that gate B already implies.

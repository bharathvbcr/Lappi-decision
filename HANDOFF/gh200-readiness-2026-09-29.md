## Throughput and caps under full vocabulary

- **What the arithmetic says about speed:**
  - The fused CE projects only chunks that hold a supervised position (one per row), so the
    wider head costs per supervised token, not per token.
  - The per-step cost that grows is the optimizer over 1.88B vs 1.40B parameters, about +34%
    of the optimizer step.
  - Expect well under 1.3× per step. That is **inferred, not measured**.
- **Size phase caps with margin anyway.** A truncated phase is a quick phase, and the phase-3
  go/no-go refuses quick rows.
  - Phase 3's `--wall-clock-cap-s` should be 1.5× its trimmed-vocab estimate: 6 h becomes 9 h
    (32,400 s).
  - Re-cut from the hour-0 tok/s row before phase 3 starts.

## The campaign config: `campaign/gh200-phase0-3.json`

- **Phases:**
  - `0-hour0` (1.5 h): the master footprint, the full-vocab phase-3 shard build, and a
    25-minute capped tok/s run. The tok/s run's ft row also sets phase 3's
    `{checkpoint_every}`.
  - `3-train` (9 h): 3 seeds, `--optimizer master`, checkpointing every 1.5 h.
  - `3-go-no-go` (1 h): three `ft_linear_control` units and the `paired_margin_vs_linear` gate
    at `min_seeds` 3.
  - The campaign cap is 13 h.
- **Validation:** `campaign_driver.py --plan` accepts it with only the three user inputs stubbed
  (scratch copy): the phases sum to $17.14, bounded at $20.49 with the 45-minute pull grace, at
  **$1.49/h, which is not yet a price**. The repo copy refuses on the empty
  `terminate_command`, as it should.
- **The trainer units accept exit 1.** That is `real_ft_run`'s "a claim failed, every row
  written"; the driver accepts it only if every row is `completed`.
- **Before launch:**
  - Fill `terminate_command`, `usd_per_hour_source`, `approved_by` and `durable_copy_dir`.
  - Set `usd_per_hour` and every unit's `--usd-per-hour` to the price-page rate.
  - Set `--rev` everywhere to the sha actually checked out on the box.
- **Phase 1** (the A2 diagnosis) and phase 2 (teacher labelling, which moved to the Mac) are not

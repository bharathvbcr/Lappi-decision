## What the subcommand applies

`qd-post-f-rules j6a --preregistration <file> --f-ledger <F> --ft-row 0= --ft-row 1= --ft-row 2=
--arm-ledger <J6(a) ledger> --j6a-ft-row 0=<id> --data-snapshot-hash <h> --shard-hash <h>
--replay-shard-hash <h> --replay-attestation-sha256 <h> --h <n> --hit-list-sha256 <h> --out <new path>`

It prints `wins`, `quiet` or `refused` on stdout. A word exits 0, `refused` exits 3, and the
JSON goes to `--out` and stderr.

1. **The pre-registration is read first.**
   - It is capped at 1 MiB, and its sha256 is recorded in `inputs` and in
     `detail.preregistration_sha256`.
   - These fields must equal the checker's, or it refuses before any ledger is read: the words,
     the targets and guards (name, direction and form, in order), and the replay flags
     (1.0, 6, `base_to_model`).
2. **The envelope, comparison and R9_room are the successor's, reused, not copied.**
   - `decide_arms` and `settle` are shared code. The envelope is F seeds 0, 1 and 2 only.
   - The comparison is strict (R7) and requires every target to clear (R3), on i128 counts.
     Room is decided from the envelope before any arm row is read.
   - No needle-control or letter-control row is read.
3. **J6(a)'s identity is its own (`replay_identity`).** `decide_arms` now takes the identity
   check as a parameter. The successor passes `arm_identity`, whose snapshot equality is
   untouched. J6(a)'s ft row must be:
   - completed, seed 0, tag `epoch`, `quick: true`, with `code_commit` a502670;
   - on build 2's pinned snapshot, which must not be F's (the envelope seed-0 ft row's);
   - on F seed 0's recipe, except for:
     - `shard_hash`, which must equal its pin;
     - exactly the five replay keys, each with its pinned or registered value, and none of
       them on F's recipe;
     - `batches` and `width`, which are recorded in the JSON and not compared (R1).
4. **The eval row** is the one completed `epoch-score-val` row for the ft row, from the arm
   ledger only (R2). Its `val_shard_hash`, `needle` and `ood` must equal F seed 0's
   (`comparable`).
5. **Words:**
   - `wins`: every target clears and no guard loses.
   - `quiet`: J6(a) was read in full and did not win.
   - `refused`: every reason is listed in `detail.refused_because`. A row, identity, data-delta,
     comparability or pin failure is (a). A target with no room is (b), shown in
     `detail.cannot_clear`.
6. **Pending amendments** refuse as (a). Room is still decided and reported. If F's envelope
   itself does not resolve, both reasons are given.

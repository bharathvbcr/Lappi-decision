### The waiter's contract

The fsucc waiter is not written yet. Words go to stdout and the JSON to `--out` and stderr. The
exit codes are:

- 0 with `fires:j6f`, `fires:j6dv4` or `quiet`;
- 3 with `refused`;
- 2 for a usage error.

The invocation:

    RULES_SUCC=/home/ubuntu/bin/qd-post-f-rules-succ
    RULES_SUCC_SHA256=ebb3975e85c61f80adc7d3f5df5240054793c39c41d6c5e988f95d91db1bfb8f
    WORD=$("$RULES_SUCC" successor --f-ledger "$F_LEDGER" \
      --ft-row 0=$(f_ft_row 0) --ft-row 1=$(f_ft_row 1) --ft-row 2=$(f_ft_row 2) \
      --arm-ledger "$J6V4_LEDGER" \
      --j6f-ft-row 0=<'ft row' id from /home/ubuntu/j6f-v4/train.log> \
      --j6dv4-ft-row 0=<'ft row' id from /home/ubuntu/j6d-v4/train.log> \
      --out "$DEC/fsucc-successor-$(date -u +%Y%m%dT%H%M%S%N).json")
    case "$WORD" in fires:j6f|fires:j6dv4|quiet) ;; *) WORD=refused ;; esac

**Rows the checker needs:**
- From F: for each of seeds 0, 1 and 2, the ft, eval, needle-control and letter-control rows.
- From J6(f): the ft, eval and needle-control rows.
- From J6(d)-v4: the ft, eval and letter-control rows. The letter-control row comes from
  `box_q_j6ctl.sh`, which is not written yet.

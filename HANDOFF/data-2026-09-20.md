## Defect 1 — nothing refused two rows whose prompts agree and whose golds disagree

`GAP-DATA-NOTHING-REFUSES-TWO-ROWS-THAT-CONTRADICT` → **resolved-with-residual**.

### Proof that the pre-fix code accepted it

The same contradictory corpus (a SQuAD pair identical in question and passage, differing only in
`is_impossible`), driven through `mixture.py` and `render.py` restored from HEAD into a scratch
tree:

```
PRE-FIX build_mixture status : ran True
PRE-FIX dedupe rows in/kept  : 178 175
PRE-FIX MixtureResult fields : [family_coverage, host_histogram, licence_histogram, n_input,
                                obligations, refusals, refused_sources, rows, status]
PRE-FIX open_training_data   : ADMITTED, n_rows = 110
PRE-FIX snapshot_status      : {'state': 'ran', 'passed': True, 'n': 3, 'n_total': 3}
```

Nine fields, none about self-consistency, and the training door opened. Post-fix the same corpus
gives `prompt_consistency = Ran(passed=False, value=2)` and `open_training_data` raises.

### The three decisions, and why

**Where: `qd_data.mixture.build_mixture`, and nowhere else.** Dedupe is the tempting seam — it
already groups these rows and already sees them — and it is the wrong one. Its key is `dedupe_text`,
which `DataRow` documents as *deliberately* not the prompt ("for a code row it is the changed code,
not the rendered prompt"), and its question is leakage. Giving it a second question with a different
key is one helper answering two with a flag meaning "do the other thing". The manifest writer is the
other candidate and sits downstream of the split, which is too late in the wrong way: a
contradictory pair split across train and val is not unlearnable, it is a guaranteed eval error, and
both are defects of the corpus rather than of a split. `build_mixture` is where a row becomes an
example and where every other "this cannot be an example" verdict already lives.

**What "the same prompt" means: the bytes `qd_data.render.render` produces at `seed=None`, hashed.**
The hard part, spelled out:

* *Not the source text.* Two rows can differ in source and render identically once escaped; the
  renderer is the normalisation.
* *Not `Request.to_wire()`.* It carries `metadata` and `expect`, which no token of the prompt
  depends on — so two rows with identical prompts can have different wire forms, and a wire-form
  hash would miss exactly the pair being hunted. (Today `Request.metadata` is always empty in this
  lane, which is what makes the shortcut tempting and wrong.)
* *`seed=None`, the serving order, no per-example shuffle.* `shuffle_options` is keyed on
  `example_id`, so grouping at a training seed produces a verdict that depends on which seed the
  shard writer happened to use — admitting at one seed a corpus it refuses at another. `seed=None`
  is stable, and it is the form `render_for_serving` already calls "the prompt for this request".
* The digest is **length-prefixed framing** over `(prefix, *slot suffixes)`, not a joined string with
  a separator. A separator is only unambiguous while nothing can emit it — true today, because
  U+0000 is in `render.HEX_ESCAPED` — and that is a property of another module's alphabet, not of
  this one.
* Known limit, recorded not hidden: `GAP-DATA-CONSISTENCY-KEY-IGNORES-OPTION-ORDER`. Two rows whose
  option *sets* agree but whose canonical *orders* differ are two prompts here. Unreachable today
  (every `ChoiceSlot` this lane builds is a fixed tuple or `sorted()`), and the error direction is a
  false negative, so it under-reports rather than refusing a legitimate corpus.

**Refuse, not report.** `MixtureResult.prompt_consistency` is folded into `MixtureResult.status`,
which aggregates into every split's manifest, and `open_training_data` refuses `ran, passed=false`
with **no override** (`allow_not_run_snapshot=True` does not open it — asserted). The manifest is
still *written*, because the row ids are the entire point: the corpus ends up legible and
untrainable rather than absent.

**The legitimate case survives by construction.** What is refused is byte-identical prompts, not the
answerable-beside-unanswerable shape the two task-holdout families depend on. SQuAD 2.0 gives its
unanswerable question different words, so the honest pair renders two prompts and never meets in a
group. `test_an_unanswerable_row_beside_an_answerable_one_is_not_refused` pins it, and also asserts
the abstaining row is still *there* — not refused away to make the check pass.

### Bounds

* `DEFAULT_MAX_CONSISTENCY_ROWS = 250_000` rows, overridable per call
  (`build_mixture(..., max_consistency_rows=)`). Measured basis: 1.88 ms/row on this repo's corpus
  (672 rows, 17.06 MB of rendered text over the 654 that render), so the bound is about eight
  minutes of rendering. Over it the pass is `NotRun` with the bound in the reason — which also
  closes the training door, because an unchecked corpus is not a clean one.
* The report payload is capped too: `MAX_NAMED_CONTRADICTIONS = 20`, `MAX_NAMED_ROW_IDS = 8`,
  `MAX_NAMED_GOLDS = 4`, with the full `n_rows` / `n_golds` / group count carried beside them. A
  pathological corpus of identical prompts with distinct golds would otherwise put megabytes of
  worked examples in a file whose job is to be read.
* Named row ids take **one witness per distinct gold first**, then fill. Naming the lowest ids
  outright can show a reader eight rows that all carry the same answer and call it the pair.

## Defect 2 — zero letter rows carry a `noul` gold

`GAP-DATA-NO-LETTER-ROW-EVER-ABSTAINS` → **open**, because the decision is not this lane's.

### The answer to the question asked

**Corpus construction, plus a structural property nobody had written down. Not weighting** —
`build_mixture` has no weights at all; it builds every registered family of every source it is
handed, once per row.

*The structural half*, read from every branch of all three rewriters: **`intent.classification` is
the only letter family that can emit `is_noul=True`, and only from a CLINC row with `is_oos`.** The
other five assign a value on every branch — `code.commit_intent` and `qa.answerability` and
`intent.in_scope` are yes/no, `code.language_id` *refuses* a row outside its closed set rather than
abstaining on it, `code.change_scope` always lands in a bin. So the letter-channel abstention supply
of any corpus is exactly its CLINC out-of-scope rows and nothing else.

*The construction half*: `tools/real_tokenizer_pipeline.py` builds only commitpackft-shaped and
squad-shaped raw rows from this repository's git history and hands `build_mixture` no
`clinc/clinc_oos` at all — so `intent.classification` is never built, and the supply is zero by
omission, not by refusal. `clinc/clinc_oos` is registered `LOADABLE` and `cc-by-3.0`; nothing
refuses it. The source registry already states its purpose: *"Its out-of-scope class is a natural,
free `noul` supervision signal"*.

| --- | --- | --- |
| choice (`code.commit_intent` 165, `code.language_id` 165, `qa.answerability` 90) | 420 | **0** |
| score (`code.change_scope` 162) | 162 | **0** |

The brief's "0 of 243" is the shard set's letter rows. The corpus-level figure is wider and the
finding is the same: **0 of 582**, not 0 of 243.

### What was built, and the line that was deliberately not crossed

`qd_data.mixture.abstention_supply(rows)` returns one `TriState` per decode channel;
`build_mixture` carries it on `MixtureResult.abstention` and into every manifest. A channel with
zero abstaining rows is `Ran(passed=False)` naming the families present, which of them can abstain
at all, and whether this is *composition* (no family on the channel can produce one) or a
*construction defect* (a family that can, produced none).

It is **reported, not folded into `MixtureResult.status`.** Whether a corpus with no letter-channel
abstention may be trained on is a kill criterion, and rule 2 puts adopting one out of an agent's
reach — the same line the MEMORY lane drew at `MAX_PADDING_WASTE`. The contradiction check *is*
folded in, and the difference is the whole point: supervision a causal model provably cannot fit is
not a threshold question.

### The two decisions waiting for a human

1. Should a corpus with no letter-channel abstention be refused at the training door, or trained
   anyway with choice/score abstention decoding reported NOT RUN?
2. If refused, the real pipeline needs a CLINC-shaped source. Note that generating abstaining letter
   rows makes the **abstain-row-LAST** invariant load-bearing —
   `crates/qd-runtime/src/answer.rs` computes `noul_row = plan.rows - RESERVED_NOUL_ROWS` in four
   places and `python/qd_train/heads.py` places it at `n_candidates`. That is BACKBONE's file, not
   this lane's, and `test_heads.py` pins it against the Rust source.

  the failing-first evidence had to be produced against a restored pre-fix tree.
* **`gaps.jsonl` suggested "group by rendered prompt hash at manifest time".** The grouping is right;
  the *place* is not, for the reason above — a pair straddling train and val is still a defect, and
  the manifest writer only ever sees one split at a time.

  engineering defaults document `.devcouncil/codeintel/devmap.sqlite`. `.devcouncil/` does exist —
  GitPulse's ledger is at `.devcouncil/ledger.sqlite` — so these are two directories, not one
  renamed.

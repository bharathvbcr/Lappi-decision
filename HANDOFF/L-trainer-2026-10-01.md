## The bug consuming the golden found

The trainer stepped the host span head on every optimizer step:
- with zero gradients on a letter-only batch, so decay was applied and the moments decayed;
- with bias corrections at the model-wide count.

torch's `zero_grad(set_to_none=True)` leaves the head's grads `None` on a letter-only batch, and
AdamW skips it. So the head keeps its own count: 13 steps of 20 in rung (b)
(`tiny-published/manifest.json` `per_parameter`). This is L-oracle's pre-registered D7, live in
the loop.

Rung (b)'s GPU parity would have failed on it. This is [inferred]; nothing was run on the GPU.
The reasoning:
- Batch 0 is letter-only, so the head's first real step is at optimizer step 1, with zero
  moments either way.
- Bias-corrected at its own t = 1, that step moves the head by lr·sign(g).
- At the model-wide t = 2 it moves by about 0.74·lr·sign(g). The difference is about
  2.6e-6 at lr ≈ 1e-5.
- The span-head bar is 1e-5 × the head's max, which is 0.125 here (`amendment2_span_head_scale`),
  so about 1.25e-6.
- The decay half of D7 alone (7 steps × lr × 0.01 × |w|) is about 1e-8·|w| and would not have
  shown.

Only the trainer's own code writes or reads its checkpoints. `rg` over `python`, `tools`,
`AUDIT`, `HANDOFF` and `crates`, and `rg -uu` over the worktree without `target/`, find
`qd-train-checkpoint`, `trainer.json` and `host.f32` only in `trainer.rs`, its tests and this
file. So moving to format v2 strands no other reader.

The fix:
- `HostParams` gradients are `Option` (torch's `None`).
- `adamw::adamw_entry_step` skips a `None` entry and bias-corrects on the entry's own count.
- The loop calls it for every host entry.
- Checkpoints carry each host entry's count (`qd-train-checkpoint-v2`; v1 is refused).

Recorded as `GAP-LTRAINER-HOST-HEAD-STEPPED-WITHOUT-GRADIENT-2026-10-02` (resolved).

The provider side is unchanged. tessl steps every tower entry on one count. The torch reference
says every tower entry has a gradient on every step (20 of 20). That ojas-qwen35 does the same
on the three span-only batches is [inferred]; it is checked when rung (b)'s parity runs.

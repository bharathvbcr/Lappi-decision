## The decision this implements

From the lead (Fable, after `HANDOFF/noul-v3-2026-10-01.md` flagged the templates):
- Still 5,004 rows = 10.02% of corpus v3's 49,953 defect rows.
- paragraph 1,005 and question 1,005, both at their ceiling of 3 per title over 335 titles.
- template 834, capped at v2's count.
- `lines` 1,080 and `tokens` 1,080: the remainder, split evenly.

Fable's reasoning, as relayed: unseen-language already took at v2's repeat level, and doubling
repeats of 35 shapes buys memorisation, a seed-variable feature of the kind that cancelled in
the J4 average. Prose (2–9/60) and scrambled (3–8/60) are where the gap is. Option (c), a
second versioned template catalogue, is for the iteration after F's readout and was not
started.

## What changed (verified)

**`qd-noul-rows generate --form-counts paragraph=N,question=N,lines=N,tokens=N,template=N`**
in `crates/qd-mutate/src/noul_rows/main.rs`:
- It is `--forms v2` only, and all five forms are required, each 1..=100,000, in any order.
- It is refused with `--per-source`, with a missing, unknown or repeated form, with a zero or
  non-numeric count, and with `--forms v1`.
- The manifest records it as `form_counts` and omits `per_source`.
- The generator fails closed if `totals.by_form` differs from what was asked.
- `--per-source` became optional (default 834, as before).
- Without `--form-counts` the layouts are unchanged. v1, v2 and v3 as committed regenerate
  byte for byte under their recorded parameters (tests below).

**Tests** (`crates/qd-mutate/tests/noul_rows.rs`):
- `form_counts_set_each_form_exactly_and_the_manifest_records_the_mix`: exact per-form counts,
  `form_counts == totals.by_form`, no `per_source`, one repo per scrambled row, and the same
  mix in another order gives identical bytes.
- `form_counts_refuse_a_mix_they_cannot_honour`: missing / unknown / repeated / zero /
  non-numeric form, with `--per-source`, beyond supply, and under v1.
- **Pre-change:** both FAILED against the unmodified generator (verified: `cargo test`, "10
  passed; 2 failed"). **Post-change:** 23 unit + 12 integration passed; clippy clean. Full
  `cargo test -p qd-mutate`: 218 passed, 0 failed across 10 test binaries.

**Python** (`python/tests/test_defect_noul.py`):
- The re-formed-corpus tests take each corpus's exact form counts and sizing flags (a
  `Reformed` record) and now include v3b.
- The real-corpus load test checks a `--form-counts` corpus against its sources' summed shares.
- **42 passed, 1 skipped in 334.7 s** (verified). The skip is the corpus-v2 shard relabel test;
  its rows are not on this worktree. Ruff is clean.

## Decisions the next reader needs

- **`--noul-weight` scope** [V]:
  - The rule lives in one place, `backbone.noul_weight_mask`. A position is weighted when:
    - the letter channel supervises it;
    - its gold is the noul letter;
    - its row's family is `code.defect_class`.
  - The weighted loss is `sum(w_i ce_i) / n_supervised`, not renormalised.
  - w=1 is bit-identical to running without the flag, in the loss and in every gradient.
  - CLINC Z rows and the span channel are not weighted.
  - The recipe keys are `noul_weight` and `noul_weight_scope`, added only when the flag is given.
  - With `--option-permutation-seed` (v5's C1) [V]: the plan counts on the unpermuted
    batches and the step on the permuted ones. A noul gold sits outside the permutation, so
    the two masks agree position for position. The toy corpus has no Z-gold row, so this is
    checked on a batch, not end to end:
    `test_noul_weight.py::test_the_option_permutation_moves_no_weighted_position`, 1 passed.
- **The Mac prelude count**:
  - [V] The prelude stops `main` by replacing `ft.Ledger`
    (`campaign/post-f-queue/j6g_prelude_mac.py:212`); it is not pinned to a line number.
  - [I] `main` builds `noul_epoch` (a `NoulWeightPlan`) before that call. So the prelude's
    frame capture can read `noul_epoch.weighted_positions` once `"noul_epoch"` is added to
    its names.
  - That prelude is not this lane's file, and this lane did not run it.
- **Retention cost** [I]:
  - The loop is handed `gcd(--checkpoint-every, N)`. At each such boundary it builds the
    step's full `state()`, about 25 GB on the 2B. The sink keeps only the tower and span head.
  - The flag commits left `trainer.py` unchanged for that reason; the retention path there is
    still unchanged. `c9d5d9c` later touched `trainer.py` for two things only: the seed-refusal
    message, and the `train.consumed_digest` metric.
  - Tracked as `GAP-V5TRAIN-RETENTION-FULL-STATE-COPY-UNMEASURED-2026-10-02`.
- **Snapshots are not resume points** [V]:
  - `_resume_arm` refuses the `-step<S>` name.
  - A resume must land on a multiple of the interval.
  - A run that would overwrite snapshots already in `--checkpoint-dir` is refused before any
    tower loads.
- **`ood_abstain.*` on trajectory rows** [V]:
  - These names come from the DRAFT's report-only reading.
  - Every consumer selects an eval row by tag or by an explicit row id:
    - `qd_post_f_rules.rs:589-623` and `:989-1011` (`Ledger::one`);
    - `qd_gate_report.rs:601`, which counts only the gate `"ood_abstain"`;
    - `tools/gate_report_row.py`, which reads the row id it is given.
  - A `trajectory-ood` row matches none of those tags. It is quick, evaluates no gate, and its
    lines are gated `ood_abstain.trajectory`.
- **Trajectory scoring cost** [I]:
  - Each invocation rebuilds the shard set, the val set and the OOD suite.
  - Tracked as `GAP-V5TRAIN-TRAJECTORY-SCORING-INVOCATION-COST-UNMEASURED-2026-10-02`.

  - `ckpt_average.read_manifest` refuses a `trajectory` manifest by name.
  - The five refusals a scorer would need relaxed are listed in
    `ckpt_average.TRAJECTORY_NOT_SCORABLE` and in
    `GAP-V5TRAIN-TRAJECTORY-AVERAGE-NOT-SCORABLE-2026-10-02`.
  - Until those relaxations are written, the v5 "one last-3 average row" cannot be produced.

  - The brief assumed `fixture_pins.rs` already pinned `trainer-oracle.json`. It did not, so

### Decisions in the build-only commits

- **`prompt_format`: absent means 1, everywhere** [V]:
  - The recipe key, the checkpoint state key, the `ckpt_average` manifest key, the Metal
    `FtRecipe.extra` and the qd-train export manifest are each written only when the format
    is not 1.
  - So v4 bytes, `recipe_hash`es and `manifest_sha256`s do not move. Tests pin the exact v4
    key sets: 18 average-manifest keys and 22 export-manifest keys.
  - The format comes from the train shard header (`reader.header.prompt_format`), never from
    `render.PROMPT_FORMAT`.
  - `_ft_row` is the one place a scored model's ft row is read. It refuses before any weights
    load, and refuses a row with no recipe.
- **`--batch-order seed`** [V] (Fable's ruling, sections 1 and 4):
  - Without the flag, every seed plans at `DataConfig().seed`, as before. The non-flag recipe
    and `recipe_hash` are proven unchanged against `f168576` in the same e2e test that shows
    the flag's orders differ by seed.
  - With the flag:
    - the recipe gains only `batch_order="seed"`, a constant, so the seeds share one
      `recipe_hash`;
    - the plan seed goes only to `reader.batches`;
    - each seed's plan is built inside the seed loop and released before the next is built
      (`real_ft_run.seed_arms` / `SeedArms.release`);
    - a seed whose plan shape differs from the first seed's is refused.
  - The flag is refused with `--score-checkpoint` and `--score-plan`.
- **Plan-order metrics on every ft row, flag or not** (the lead's ruling) [V]:
  - `corpus.plan_seed` and `corpus.plan_order_digest` are written by `real_ft_run._train`.
    The digest is `real_ft_run.plan_order_digest`: sha256 over the compact JSON of the plan's
    `[[bucket, [rows]], ...]`.
  - `train.consumed_digest` is written by `qd_train.trainer._train_loop`, from the final
    checkpoint.
    - That checkpoint is built unconditionally at `trainer.py:897`.
    - Its digest is a `ConsumedPrefix` hexdigest (`:686`). On a zero-step run, it is the
      digest of what was consumed: a value, never `None`.
- **The Rust readers of ft rows accept the extra keys** [V, lines at `c9d5d9c`; v5-build's
  L-lint reflow may move them]:
  - `qd_post_f_rules.rs`:
    - `Row { map: Map<String, Value> }` at `:400-403`; `parse_rows` at `:476-497`;
    - the only walk over metric keys is `needle_worst` at `:696-713`, which reads eval rows by
      the needle prefix.
  - `qd_gate_report.rs`: `json_lines` into `Map<String, Value>` at `:261` and `:300-317`.
  - `qd-train/src/ledger.rs`: reads earlier lines only as bytes, for the chain hash (`:665`).
  - `deny_unknown_fields` appears only on wire types.
  - **For L-v5-rules:** `qd_post_f_rules.rs` compares recipe keys exhaustively at `:1788`,
    `:2456`, `:2720` and `:2758`. A v5 `batch_order` key will differ between an arm and F.
- **`test_batch_order.py`'s `BASE_REF`** [V]:
  - It is `f168576`, read with `git show`, so the test needs that commit in its history.
  - When the non-flag recipe legitimately moves, refresh `BASE_REF` to the commit below that

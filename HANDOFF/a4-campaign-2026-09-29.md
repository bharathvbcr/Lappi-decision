## What is open

* **Verdicts are not on disk.** `real_ft_run.py` must add `--verdicts-out` (contract sent to
  the lane that owns it: `eval_row_id`, `seed`, `row_id`, `kind`, `correct`, optional
  `slot_name`) and `ft_split_rows(*, commitpackft, max_pairs, rev, config)`. Until both land,
  `ft_linear_control.py` refuses and a phase-3 go/no-go cannot pass. Historical FT rows
  cannot be retrofitted.
* **Operator holdout on real data** needs A3's family to carry `metadata["operator"]` and
  `real_ft_run.py` to accept and record `hold_out_operator` in its recipe.
* **Box → Mac transport: decided 2026-09-29 (lead), the Mac pulls; built.** The driver
  writes an immutable `sync/phase-<n>/` snapshot + `phase-<n>.done` (sha256 manifest; ledgers
  copied, checkpoints hard-linked, state + unit logs included) after every phase and does
  not wait. `sync_box.sh pull` (rewritten; the old ledger-dir pull is gone) verifies each
  snapshot against its manifest and writes `pulled-<n>.ok` back; `campaign_pull_loop.sh`
  loops it on the Mac. Every termination — end, stop, failure, the guard at the cap,
  `--finalize` — is gated on the final `pulled-<n>.ok`; else it waits `pull_grace_minutes`
  (default 45, included in the up-front cost), then terminates only on a verified
  `durable_copy_dir` (other device + every sha256), else keeps the box alive (exit 4) and
  prints $/h. `sync_command` is now refused. Lambda's persistent-filesystem mount path is
  not yet known here: `durable_copy_dir` is unset until someone confirms it on the box.
* The real Lambda terminate command and `usd_per_hour_source` (read from the price page at
  launch) must be filled in the campaign config; neither is in the repo.
* **Within-unit resume was not exercised end to end**: the stand-in refuses
  `--checkpoint-dir`, so the smoke's kill + resume is unit-granular. The `--resume-from`
  wiring is covered by `test_checkpoint_cadence_is_derived_from_measured_throughput` only.
* A unit that uses `real_ft_run --seeds 0 1 2` in one invocation and is killed reruns all
  three; put one seed per unit. The driver refuses two units sharing a checkpoint dir, and
  refuses a `real_ft_run.py --real-backbone` unit with no explicit `--optimizer` (the GPU
  lane's finding: `optim.py` refuses bf16 on long schedules; use `master`).
* The `sync_box.sh pull` copy+verify test skips (NOT RUN) whenever any training process is
  live on the host, because the pull correctly refuses; the smoke's phase-0 and phase-1
  pulls did run and verify.

**Every training call** (v5 s0-s4, the arm, J5′) is `tools/real_ft_run.py "${V5_SPLIT[@]}"
"${V5_RECIPE[@]}" …`. `V5_RECIPE` is built by `v5_recipe` from the pins:

It also prints, report-only, each ft row's `corpus.plan_order_digest` beside the prelude's for
that plan seed (`MATCHES` / `DIFFERS FROM`); nothing branches on it.

**The rules.** Every call goes through `v5_rule` (a reading) or `v5_rules_call` (`ft-rows`,

not used and not replaced; `post_f_common.sh rule()` is not used. A word is read only as exit 0

(section 2, reading 7). Each attempt's `--out` JSON and stderr are kept in `$DEC_V5` as
`<label>-<UTC>-try<k>.{json,stderr}`. The command lines are the v5-rules handoff's section 7,
with `--f-ledger "$V5_LEDGER"` for `seeds34`.

**Money.** `v5_read_prereg` reads, from the pinned pre-registration: the per-seed figures in
`launch.projected_cost_usd.check` (a v5 / seeds 3-4 / arm seed ~ 7.0 h, $16.0; a J5′ seed ~ 6.0

`$Q/v5.spend`, and the running total prints after each. Before each training run,
`v5_budget_ok` refuses a run whose estimate would carry the total past the approved figure
(USD or GPU-h; + `if_seeds_3_4` once `v5s34.word` is `fires`) unless `$Q/V5_OVER_BUDGET_YES`
holds the human's words (the lead's instruction, 2026-10-02). The per-run caps are unchanged;
no threshold or gate was moved.

**Fail-closed pins.** While any pin is `UNSET` every waiter logs `deferred: pins UNSET` and
exits 3 without touching `gpu.lock` (its `.done` is touched, as in j6a/j6g). Each filled pin is
checked for its form (64-hex sha, 40-hex commit, YYYY-MM-DD, its decision words). After the
waits and before the lock, `v5_verify` checks: both binaries' sha256, the lane clean at its
full commit, the pre-registration's sha256 and figures, a v5 train header in `--out` and **no**
`data/heldout` under it (rule 3), the box tokenizer, and the prelude record (its sha256 and
fields). `V5_SPLIT` accepts data flags only (an allowlist), one absolute `--out`, one full
`--rev`, the box backbone, and no argv word naming held-out data or the containment request.
v5 refuses an existing v5 ledger and stale `V5_CONTINUE` / `V5_STOP` / `v5.paused`; the arm
refuses an existing arm ledger; a decision word file that exists is never re-decided.

   waiter may still append to the same ledger: `v5-noulw` (the arm's reading) right after arm

   selects rows by run kind and tag, so those rows do not count [V, `Ledger::one` read]. A read
   that meets a half-written last line refuses [V: the v5 build, run on such a ledger, printed
   it; `AUDIT/v5-queue-2026-10-02/read-race-probe.txt`]. The waiters do **not** wait for
   `<run>traj-s2.done`: that waiter releases `gpu.lock` before its CPU controls, so waiting on it
   would idle the GPU for the whole control phase. Instead `v5_rules_run` makes the call again
   only when all four hold:
   - the binary exited 3 and printed `refused`;
   - its last stderr line is `qd-post-f-rules: refused: <L> line <N>: malformed ledger line

   - `<N>` is `<L>`'s last line now, counted as `parse_rows` counts (newlines, plus one when the

   writes (`python/qd_train/ledger.py`, `_take_write_lock`), so a `flock -s` around each read
   would close the race at its source; not done, the ruling is the retry.

## 5. The prelude, `v5_prelude_mac.py`

j6g_prelude_mac.py's form. It runs `real_ft_run.main` from a clean checkout on v5 seed 0's
training argv and stops at `Ledger(args.ledger)` (before the ledger opens and before any tower
loads), takes main's locals from its frame there, and then calls the tool's own code:

- `plan_seed_for("seed", ...)` and `plan_order_digest` (the epoch plan, and arm 2's cut at
  `--max-width`) for plan seeds 0-4;
- `seed_arms(...)` for plan seeds 1-4, one plan at a time beside main's seed-0 plan, released
  after use; `SeedArms.shape()` must equal main's, and each `SeedArms` digest must equal
  `plan_order_digest`'s;
- `noul_weight_plan` over main's seed-0 epoch plan at `--noul-weight W` (the arm's weight; main
  itself runs without it): the weighted and supervised letter positions per pass and the mass
  ratio;
- with `--option-permutation-seed` (C1): `_train`'s validation loop (`permutation.apply` per
  batch, alphabets from `_alphabets`) over every seed's plan, and the "Option permutation seed
  …" sentence.

It refuses (no record): an argv that is not v5 seed 0's (2), a main that refuses or never reaches
the ledger or runs without `--batch-order seed` (3), a `PermutationRefusal` (4), a shape that
moves with the seed, two seeds sharing a digest, or main's plan not being plan seed 0's (5). On
exit 0 it writes the record once (`O_EXCL`, fsync) and prints `RECORD <path> sha256 <hex>` and
`PRELUDE OK`. The record carries the fields `v5_common.sh v5_prelude_check` reads: `ok`,
`code_commit` (the checkout's HEAD), `argv`, `batch_order`, `plan_order_digest` and
`plan_order_digest_small` (plan seeds `"0"`-`"4"`), `shape`, `shape_equal_across_seeds`, the
plan's batches / widest / rows / padded positions, `shard_hash`, `data_snapshot_hash`,
`tokenizer_json_sha256`, `option_permutation_seed`, `sentence`, and the noul-weight counts.

It has run only on the toy corpus (section 3); on v5's real plan it has not run
(GAP-V5-QUEUE-PRELUDE-NOT-RUN-ON-V5-DATA-2026-10-02). The command is in section 7.

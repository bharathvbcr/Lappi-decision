1. Train + score: `--checkpoint-dir <ckpt> --checkpoint-every 100000 --retain-tower-every 1000

## 2. Readings and deviations (lead to confirm; GAP-V5-QUEUE-DESIGN-READINGS-2026-10-02)

1. R9 `refused`, an unknown word, or no seed-0 ft row hold exactly like `pause` (the DRAFT names
   only pause).
2. A refused `--room` never runs the arm, even with `V5NW_HUMAN_YES`. The yes covers `no_room`.
   The arm's own reading decides room from the same envelope first, so a refused room makes
   every arm row read refused: about $48 for nothing.
3. The post-seed waiter is started by its parent, not launched by hand, and the parent hands it
   the lock. The DRAFT says "a separate waiter per seed … holding gpu.lock"; this is that, with
   the hand-off made deterministic (flock is not FIFO). It also runs the CPU controls once the
   lock is released.
4. The running total counts GPU-step wall time, not idle box time (an R9 hold, the waits). The
   box bills those hours too.
5. The budget stop compares the estimate, not the cap, and both USD and GPU-h bind. At $2.29/h a
   7.0 h seed costs $16.03 against the DRAFT's $16.0, so near the end of the block the USD side
   binds first (`test_v5_budget_ok_passes_inside_the_approved_total`: 53 h spent refuses a 7 h
   seed on USD while 60 h does not cross).
6. The ledger rows' own `cost_usd` was not used for the total: the eval row's wall clock
   undercounts the score phase (GAP-EVAL-ROW-WALL-CLOCK-UNDERCOUNTS-SCORE-PHASE-2026-10-02), and
   the box bills the invocation's whole wall time.
7. A build flag outside the `V5_SPLIT` allowlist (`--out --no-repo-history --rev --defect-class
   --defect-download --defect-max-rows --defect-noul --general-record --general-max-rows
   --exclude-identity-keys --real-backbone --commitpackft --max-pairs`) refuses until the
   allowlist is amended.

## 5. The prelude, `v5_prelude_mac.py`

j6g_prelude_mac.py's form. It runs `real_ft_run.main` from a clean checkout on v5 seed 0's
training argv and stops at `Ledger(args.ledger)` (before the ledger opens and before any tower
loads), takes main's locals from its frame there, and then calls the tool's own code:

- `plan_seed_for("seed", ...)` and `plan_order_digest` (the epoch plan, and arm 2's cut at
  `--max-width`) for plan seeds 0-4;
- `seed_arms(...)` for plan seeds 1-4, one plan at a time beside main's seed-0 plan, released
  after use; `SeedArms.shape()` must equal main's, and each `SeedArms` digest must equal
  `plan_order_digest`'s;
- `noul_weight_plan` over main's seed-0 epoch plan at `--noul-weight W` (the arm's weight; main
  itself runs without it): the weighted and supervised letter positions per pass and the mass
  ratio;
- with `--option-permutation-seed` (C1): `_train`'s validation loop (`permutation.apply` per
  batch, alphabets from `_alphabets`) over every seed's plan, and the "Option permutation seed
  …" sentence.

It refuses (no record): an argv that is not v5 seed 0's (2), a main that refuses or never reaches
the ledger or runs without `--batch-order seed` (3), a `PermutationRefusal` (4), a shape that
moves with the seed, two seeds sharing a digest, or main's plan not being plan seed 0's (5). On
exit 0 it writes the record once (`O_EXCL`, fsync) and prints `RECORD <path> sha256 <hex>` and
`PRELUDE OK`. The record carries the fields `v5_common.sh v5_prelude_check` reads: `ok`,
`code_commit` (the checkout's HEAD), `argv`, `batch_order`, `plan_order_digest` and
`plan_order_digest_small` (plan seeds `"0"`-`"4"`), `shape`, `shape_equal_across_seeds`, the
plan's batches / widest / rows / padded positions, `shard_hash`, `data_snapshot_hash`,
`tokenizer_json_sha256`, `option_permutation_seed`, `sentence`, and the noul-weight counts.

It has run only on the toy corpus (section 3); on v5's real plan it has not run
(GAP-V5-QUEUE-PRELUDE-NOT-RUN-ON-V5-DATA-2026-10-02). The command is in section 7.

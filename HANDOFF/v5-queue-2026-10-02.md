**Every training call** (v5 s0-s4, the arm, J5′) is `tools/real_ft_run.py "${V5_SPLIT[@]}"
"${V5_RECIPE[@]}" …`. `V5_RECIPE` is built by `v5_recipe` from the pins:

**Per seed of v5, seeds 3-4 and the arm** (`v5_train_seed`, `v5_needle_control`,
`v5_post_seed`):

1. Train + score: `--checkpoint-dir <ckpt> --checkpoint-every 100000 --retain-tower-every 1000

   --score-val --needle --needle-control 1024,2048,4096 --score-dtype fp32`, cap 5,400 s.
3. The post-seed waiter `box_q_v5traj.sh RUN SEED FT` is started (`setsid nohup`, fd 9 closed
   for it), the parent releases `gpu.lock`, and waits at most 900 s for its `.started` or

that plan seed (`MATCHES` / `DIFFERS FROM`); nothing branches on it.

## 2. Readings and deviations (lead to confirm; GAP-V5-QUEUE-DESIGN-READINGS-2026-10-02)

1. R9 `refused`, an unknown word, or no seed-0 ft row hold exactly like `pause` (the DRAFT names
   only pause).
2. A refused `--room` never runs the arm, even with `V5NW_HUMAN_YES`. The yes covers `no_room`.
   The arm's own reading decides room from the same envelope first, so a refused room makes
   every arm row read refused: about $48 for nothing.
3. The post-seed waiter is started by its parent, not launched by hand, and the parent hands it
   the lock. The DRAFT says "a separate waiter per seed … holding gpu.lock"; this is that, with
   the hand-off made deterministic (flock is not FIFO). It also runs the CPU controls once the
   lock is released.
4. The running total counts GPU-step wall time, not idle box time (an R9 hold, the waits). The
   box bills those hours too.
5. The budget stop compares the estimate, not the cap, and both USD and GPU-h bind. At $2.29/h a
   7.0 h seed costs $16.03 against the DRAFT's $16.0, so near the end of the block the USD side
   binds first (`test_v5_budget_ok_passes_inside_the_approved_total`: 53 h spent refuses a 7 h
   seed on USD while 60 h does not cross).
6. The ledger rows' own `cost_usd` was not used for the total: the eval row's wall clock
   undercounts the score phase (GAP-EVAL-ROW-WALL-CLOCK-UNDERCOUNTS-SCORE-PHASE-2026-10-02), and
   the box bills the invocation's whole wall time.
7. A build flag outside the `V5_SPLIT` allowlist (`--out --no-repo-history --rev --defect-class
   --defect-download --defect-max-rows --defect-noul --general-record --general-max-rows
   --exclude-identity-keys --real-backbone --commitpackft --max-pairs`) refuses until the
   allowlist is amended.

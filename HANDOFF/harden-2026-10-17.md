## Lessons this pass repeated, so the next lane does not

- **Stale lane bases.** Worktree isolation cuts from origin/main, which is 270+ behind local main. Every lane prompt must switch to an explicit sha and check `merge-base --is-ancestor` as step 0.
- **Truncated gap ids,** 4 times. `~/.ripgreprc` truncates lines at 250 columns: use `RIPGREP_CONFIG_PATH= rg`. test_gaps_ledger catches a bad citation; run it before every commit that cites an id.
- **Relaying a ruling before it is committed.** The peer rightly refused MAX_LOAD=48 until it saw the file:line.
- **Untracked files in the repo tree,** e.g. `tmp/` is not ignored. They fail test_gaps_ledger and the lint gate.
